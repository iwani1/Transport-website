How it works

From port gate
to your door.

You send one message. We do the paperwork, the port, the trucks and the storage — and you get one invoice at the end.

  1. Send the details

    Share your bill of lading or airway bill, the invoice and the delivery address — by WhatsApp, e-mail or the form below.

    Within the hour

  2. Get a fixed quote

    Duties, port fees, transport and storage in one itemised quote. No surprises at the gate.

    Same day

  3. We clear & move it

    Our team handles customs and the authorities; our trucks collect the moment it is released.

    2 – 5 days

  4. Delivered

    Signed delivery, photos on request, invoice by e-mail. Storage in Tema if you need it.

    Done
